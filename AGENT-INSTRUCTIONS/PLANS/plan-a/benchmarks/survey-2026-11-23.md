# Closing survey - plan-a (materials)

**Date:** 2026-11-23
**Branch:** `plan-a`, surveyed head `dca633c`
**Plan scope:** materials and how rays scatter: diffuse, metal and glass, the bounce limit, samples
per pixel and gamma correction (`docs/requirements.md` items 1 to 6).
**Earlier survey note:** none. This plan has no previous survey.

## Areas surveyed

1. Material trait and the three materials - `src/material.rs`
2. Hit record, constructor and scene list - `src/hittable.rs`
3. Sphere and material wiring - `src/sphere.rs`
4. Scene of four spheres - `scene()` in `src/main.rs`
5. Scatter loop and bounce limit - `ray_colour` in `src/main.rs`
6. Samples per pixel - `render` and `SAMPLES` in `src/main.rs`
7. Gamma correction and PPM output - `to_byte`, `write_ppm`, `main` in `src/main.rs`
8. Seeded randomness and reproducibility - `src/rng.rs` and every generator use
9. Tests across the plan - all twelve test functions
10. Permanent documentation and the repository map

Each area was read by one independent read-only scout; one further independent scout cross-checked
every claim below against the source and the documents. All verdicts are from that cross-check.

Two claims from the area surveys were rejected by the cross-check and are not listed above: that
`to_byte` is the only undocumented function in `src/main.rs` (three others are undocumented too, and
no rule requires per-function docs), and that the diffuse material can scatter below the surface
(it cannot: `(normal + r)·normal = 1 + r·n >= 0` for a unit normal and unit `r`).

## Out-of-scope observations

Kept separate; none was investigated further and none is this plan's work.

- PNG output, the `--width`/`--height`/`--samples`/`--output`/`--format` command line and the
  progress line in `docs/requirements.md` items 11 to 12 are unimplemented on this branch by design
  (output plan); `main` writes PPM to standard output.
- Planes, triangles, bounding boxes and the `front_face`/`u`/`v` record fields (requirements 7 to 10)
  arrive from the geometry plan at the merge, per `PLANS/shared/plan-a-A1+plan-b-B2.md`.
- The scene's ground is a radius-100 sphere rather than the infinite plane of requirement 9; whoever
  reconciles the merge decides whether the fixed scene keeps the sphere.
- `done/A2-glass.md` says the tests pin Schlick's "about 0.04 ... about 0.65"; the tests assert
  direction outcomes, so those numbers are exercised rather than asserted.
- `scene()` is rebuilt on every `render` call; harmless at this size.

## Checks performed

- **Requirements 1 to 4:** the frozen contracts in `done/A1r-hit-record-constructor-and-materials.md`
  and `done/A2-glass.md` were compared with the current source and match; their tests were not rerun.
- **Requirement 5:** the A3 contract holds in `src/main.rs:24,84-89,96` - the default is 16, each
  pixel is the mean of jittered samples from one generator, and `to_byte` is the only place gamma 2
  is applied.
- **Requirement 6 and the hard invariants:** `Cargo.toml` has an empty `[dependencies]`; every draw
  comes from the one seeded `src/rng.rs` threaded from `render` through `ray_colour` into the
  materials, with no reseed and no second source; `src/` contains no `unsafe` and no thread type.
- **Requirement 14 and the fast gate:** the receipt at `dca633c` reports `cargo build`, `cargo test`
  (12 passed), `cargo clippy -- -D warnings`, `cargo fmt --check` and `git diff --check`; not rerun on
  the unchanged revision.
- **Test budget:** seven test functions added across the plan (three in A1r, three in A2, one in A3),
  never more than three per phase; none deleted and no assertion weakened.
- **Documentation accuracy:** recorded as findings F1, F2, F14 and the out-of-scope observations.
- **Reproducibility:** the scene was rendered twice at 200×100; `cmp` reports the two files
  byte-identical. The image has 20003 lines and begins `P3`, `200 100`, `255`.
- **Rendered image:** converted to PNG and opened. Sky gradient, yellow-green ground, matte
  red-brown centre sphere, glass sphere on the left with the bent, inverted sky and ground inside it
  and a bright rim, grey metal sphere on the right reflecting the scene; edges smooth at 16 samples.
  Kept for the user at `/tmp/opencode/survey-a.png` (and `/tmp/opencode/survey-a.ppm`).
